# Comprehensive Competitor Feature Matrix: PDF24 vs. iLovePDF vs. ErgonPDF

## Methodology & Evidence Standard
This feature matrix evaluates the functional availability, implementation fidelity, and limitations across **PDF24**, **iLovePDF**, and **ErgonPDF**.

### Status Standard
- **Available**: Fully implemented, functional, and verified in production/code.
- **Partially Available**: Implemented with notable functional limits, degraded fidelity, or restricted tiers.
- **Unavailable**: Missing or completely unhandled by the platform.
- **Unverified**: Claimed in marketing or UI, but unable to independently verify execution.
